# Mutual NDA for an Open Ecosystem Success Story

A very basic template. Fill in the brackets, check it against your own law, and have it reviewed if it matters to you. It isn't legal advice.

Every party of a Story signs the same version with the author.

---

Between **[Author: name, legal entity, address]** ("the Author")
and **[Party: name, legal entity, address]** ("the Party")
for the Open Ecosystem Success Story about **[project]** ("the Story").

**1. Purpose.** We share information so the Story can be researched, written and published. Publishing it is the goal of this agreement.

**2. What's confidential.** Non-public information one of us marks as confidential when sharing it, or confirms as confidential in writing within 14 days. Nothing else. That we're working on the Story together is not confidential.

**3. The shared document.** The Story is written in one shared document that every party of the Story can read and write into. What any party writes there is confidential for everyone until the Story is published.

**4. How we handle it.** Only for the purpose above. Protected as carefully as our own. Shared only with humans who need it and are bound to the same terms.

**5. What's not covered.** Information that's public without our fault, that we already knew, that we got from someone else without restriction, or that we developed ourselves. Either of us may disclose what the law requires.

**6. Your part.** The Party writes its own section and its own quote. Nothing about the Party is published without its written approval. Silence never counts as approval.

**7. After publication.** What's published is no longer confidential.

**8. Duration.** [three] years from signing. Either of us can stop sharing at any time.

**9. Nothing else.** No exclusivity, no obligation to share anything, no obligation to publish. Either of us stays free to work with anyone, competitors included.

**10. Law.** [Governing law]. Place of jurisdiction: [place].

Email counts as writing.
