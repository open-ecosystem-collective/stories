# Open Ecosystem Success Stories

Most case studies are written by one vendor about its own part. The partners who built the rest stay invisible, or show up as a logo at the bottom.

An Open Ecosystem Success Story is different. It's one joint story about one project, written in one shared document. Every company that built the project writes its own part into it, the customer included, and it only publishes when all of them say yes.

## The one rule

**All or nothing.** Every named party approves the Story and every channel, or it doesn't publish anywhere. Nobody is left out to make it easier, and nobody who declines gets named.

That's the only rule. Everything else is self-accountability.

## How a Story gets here

1. The author writes a base story from the traces the project left, and draws the ecosystem map.
2. The customer reads it first, checks it's complete and decides within two weeks whether the project may become a Story.
3. The customer introduces the author to every partner. The author then sends every partner the same mail on the same day.
4. Each partner says in or out within two weeks, then gets at least two months to write its own section and quote.
5. Every party approves every channel in the shared document and signs the Publication Consent.
6. The Story publishes on all channels on the same day: as a blog post, in print and online media, and here.

The version in this repository is the canonical one. It names who wrote it and who signed it off, so the neutrality can be checked by anyone.
